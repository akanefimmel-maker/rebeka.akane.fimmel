PROFILE

Rebeka Akane Fimmel (born 1998 in Dresden) is an artist working between sculpture and performance currently based in Halle(Saale) Germany.

The artist draws thematically from her multi-cultural backround of growing up in Eastern Germany with a Japanese Father. Her expressions are shifting depending on the context and the materials available at the site. The artist aims to create an environment, in which new forms of communication between audience and artwork can unfold.

Dust in the Stomach

Dust in the Stomach, 2026
"Dust in the Stomach" is a Performance/Installation that was shown at SSK Gallery in Osaka in 2026. This work is a collaboration with the performance artist Karu Miyoshi. Visitors enter the exhibtion space alone, to be seperated from external view. In this scenario they are able to experience the artwork in an intimate setting. Inside of the room are architectural elements, made out of soil and ceramics, as well as the relief of human body. The visitor is allowed to touch the installation.

TALE AS OLD AS TIME,DIGGING ON SALTY GROUNDS

"Tale as old as Time, Digging on Salty Grounds",is a performative walk on the mid eval castle site of Burg Giebichenstein Castle
in Halle, Saxony-Anahlt. The artist is presenting the walk in the context of the ending of decades of coal mining in Eastern Germany, reflecting on it's impact on workers.
