PROFILE

Rebeka Akane Fimmel (born 1998 in Dresden) is a interdisciplinary artist working between Germany and Japan. Her sculptural work is closely linked to material and site specific research.

The artist draws thematically from her cultural backround of Eastern Germany as well as her japanese roots, creating a shifting expressions depending on the context. In this environment communication becomes a crucial factor in exhibting art.
